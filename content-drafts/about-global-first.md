**Lede:** Tilth is a growth marketing agency for ambitious brands — wherever they're scaling. We're founder-led and India-rooted, fluent in how demanding markets like the US buy and convert, and built on one belief: growth is a consequence of strong foundations, not more spend.

### Why Tilth exists
**Label:** Why Tilth exists
**H2:** The same mistake, repeated across every industry.
**Body:** Across fitness, edtech, fintech, SaaS and D2C — and across markets — one pattern kept repeating: teams scaling spend on foundations that couldn't carry it. Broken tracking. Unclear positioning. Sites that didn't convert. Tilth exists to fix that first, so growth compounds instead of leaking.

### Positioning  ← the requested angle
**Label:** How we're built
**H2:** Global-first. US-aware. India-rooted.
**Intro:** Three ideas, one operating stance.

- **Global-first.** We work with brands wherever growth is happening. The method isn't tied to a geography — it's tied to a question: can the foundation carry the spend? That holds true in any market.
- **US-aware.** We understand how buyers in demanding markets like the US research, convert and churn — and we build funnels, creative and measurement that stand up to that scrutiny.
- **India-rooted.** Tilth is built and based in India, registered in Bengaluru. That keeps senior work close, fast and accountable — at a level of craft and value that global teams notice.

### How we work
**Label:** How we work
**H2:** Senior, accountable, distributed.
- **Founder-led direction** — Senior strategy on every engagement. You're not handed to a junior team after the pitch.
- **Built for distributed teams** — Remote-first collaboration with overlap that keeps work moving, wherever your team sits.
- **Foundation-first** — We diagnose before we prescribe, and only scale once the growth system can carry it.
- **One accountable partner** — Strategy, execution and measurement coordinated through Tilth, not scattered across vendors.
