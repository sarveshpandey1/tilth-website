Questions

Growth Strategy & Measurement, answered.

Is this a one-off audit or an ongoing engagement?

It can be either. Many teams start with a fixed-scope Foundation Audit, then continue into an ongoing partnership to implement and refine the measurement system.

Do you implement the tracking, or just advise?

Both. We define the measurement strategy and can implement the tracking, conversion events and reporting, then validate that the data is accurate before you rely on it.

We already have analytics set up. Why do we need this?

Most accounts have analytics installed but not aligned to revenue — events fire, but they don't answer business questions. We check whether your data can actually support budget decisions, and fix it where it can't.
