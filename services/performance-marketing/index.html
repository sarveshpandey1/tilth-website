Questions

Performance Marketing, answered.

What's the difference between performance marketing and paid media?

Paid media is the execution on platforms like Google and Meta. Performance marketing is the wider system — strategy, economics, experimentation and measurement — that decides whether that spend scales profitably.

Will you fix tracking before scaling spend?

Yes. Scaling a channel with broken measurement just buys more unreliable data. We confirm the numbers are trustworthy first, then scale.

Can you work alongside our internal team?

Yes. We often provide senior strategy and direction while an internal team executes, or run execution end to end — whichever fits.
