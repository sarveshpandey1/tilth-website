Questions

Affiliate & Partnership Marketing, answered.

Can you build a program from scratch?

Yes. We've built affiliate programs from zero — model, partners, tracking and controls — and grown them into a meaningful share of revenue.

How do you prevent low-quality or fraudulent activity?

Through validated partners, payout structures tied to quality outcomes, and tracking with fraud safeguards — so the program rewards real customers.

Is this only for large companies?

No. A well-designed program can be one of the most capital-efficient channels for a growing business, precisely because you pay for results.
