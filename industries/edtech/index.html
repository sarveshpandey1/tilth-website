The context

Growth constraints in Edtech.

Edtech can scale fast — and waste fast. The brands that win build measurement that keeps up with the spend, so growth is efficient rather than just large.

Spend scales quickly but efficiency drops as it grows.
Enrolment reporting doesn't reconcile with marketing data.
Creative fatigues fast and testing isn't structured.
Seasonal demand makes budgeting and forecasting hard.
Lead quality varies widely across channels.
