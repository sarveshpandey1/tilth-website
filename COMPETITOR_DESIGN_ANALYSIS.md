# COMPETITOR_DESIGN_ANALYSIS.md — Tilth

Structural + conversion lessons only (Brief §45). **Do not copy** layout, copy, colours, components or identity — extract patterns and apply them through Tilth's own Living Soil system. Observations reflect the common patterns of these agencies' marketing sites; re-verify against the live sites when validating.

## Sites reviewed
NoGood · Wpromote · Power Digital · Siege Media · WebFX

## Cross-cutting patterns worth adopting
| Pattern | Seen at | Tilth application |
|---|---|---|
| **Proof above the fold or immediately after** — logos/results before services | Wpromote, Power Digital, WebFX | Trust markers placed immediately after hero (done); anonymous result snapshots high on home + `/work/`. |
| **Outcome-led service naming** (growth/acquisition/revenue), not just channel names | NoGood, Power Digital | Services framed by outcome (Growth Strategy & Measurement, Performance Marketing…) with buyer-problem intros. |
| **Named, repeatable methodology** as a trust device | NoGood, Power Digital | The Tilth Cycle, now with plain-language phases + deliverables. |
| **Results/case-study system** with metric-forward cards | all five | `/work/` + case-study data model; metric-forward proof cards (revenue/ROAS/efficiency, not clicks). |
| **Industry/vertical pages** to signal domain fit | Wpromote, WebFX, Power Digital | `/industries/` hub + 5 sector pages with real sector problems/metrics. |
| **Content/SEO depth as authority** (large insights libraries, tools) | Siege Media, WebFX | Existing 17 insights + 7 tools; add US/global commercial articles. |
| **Restrained, consistent motion**; clarity over spectacle | Siege Media, NoGood (tasteful) | One motion idea per screen; reduced-motion honored; light/dark balance. |
| **Intent-based CTAs** (talk to us / see work / audit), not one generic CTA | all five | Discuss a Project / Request a Foundation Audit / See Our Work / Explore the Service. |
| **Clear nav with Work + Industries + Services** | Wpromote, WebFX, Power Digital | New IA adds Work + Industries (absence previously hurt credibility). |
| **Light + dark section rhythm** for scannability | NoGood, Siege Media | 60/30/10 dark/cream/accent; cream service, industries, insight/tool sections. |

## Differentiators Tilth should keep (do NOT converge to the category)
- Category norm is bright, blue/neon, dense, SaaS-generic. Tilth's **Living Soil** (soil-black, cream, sage, terracotta; editorial serif) is a genuine differentiator — **preserve it**.
- Foundation-first positioning ("we don't start by recommending another channel") is a sharper POV than the typical "full-funnel/data-driven" claims — lead with it, but show it through services/process/proof rather than repeating the manifesto.

## Anti-patterns observed — avoid
- Generic superlatives ("results-driven", "full-funnel", "data-driven solutions") — Tilth's banned-phrase list already blocks these.
- Wall-to-wall logos/marquees with no substance; fabricated or vague metrics.
- Over-animation that delays comprehension; hero text far from the eye line.
- Thin programmatic city/industry pages for SEO with no original content.

## Net takeaways applied in this build
1. Proof earlier and metric-forward. 2. Work + Industries in IA. 3. Outcome-led services with dedicated pages. 4. Methodology made concrete. 5. Intent CTAs. 6. Light/dark rhythm. 7. Keep the distinctive brand — do not look like a blue SaaS agency.
