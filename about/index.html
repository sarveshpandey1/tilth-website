About

Built by someone who's done the work.

Tilth is a performance-led growth marketing agency. Founder-led, with 10+ years of hands-on growth experience — India-rooted, with performance experience across India and the US — and built on one belief: growth is a consequence of strong foundations, not more spend.

Why Tilth exists

The same mistake, repeated across every industry.

Across more than ten industries, in India and the US, one pattern kept repeating: teams scaling spend on foundations that couldn't support it — broken tracking, unclear positioning, sites that didn't convert. Tilth exists to fix that first, so growth compounds instead of leaking.
