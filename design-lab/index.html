TILTH · CONCEPT LAB
Phase 0 · Internal exploration · Not final

Three worlds Tilth could grow into.

Each direction is a genuinely different, complete brand world — same honest positioning, three ways to express it. The final site may combine selected elements from an approved concept. No winner is chosen here.

Umbrella direction: “Editorial intelligence built on living systems.” Every concept supports light and dark themes, respects reduced motion, and uses only approved text, names and anonymous proof. Toggle the theme (top-right) on any page.

Concept A

Living Intelligence

Organic systems becoming measurable intelligence — roots resolving into an ordered growth system.

Audience perception
Premium, distinctive, quietly sophisticated.
Visual principle
Organic network → structured nodes; Living Soil palette.
Motion principle
“Root → Signal” hero that settles into order.
Strength
Most ownable / on-brand; memorable.
Risk
Can drift decorative if motion isn’t tied to meaning.
Needs custom imagery
Token-driven system SVGs (L/D), founder portrait.
