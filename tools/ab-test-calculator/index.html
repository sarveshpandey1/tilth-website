The chance this difference is real, not random. A common bar to act is 95%+.

Confidence isn't the whole story. Even at 95%, run the test for full business cycles (weekdays and weekends behave differently), don't stop the moment it crosses the line, and make sure both variants got enough conversions — a handful of conversions can look significant and still be a fluke. Statistics tells you the difference is probably real; it can't tell you it's worth shipping.
