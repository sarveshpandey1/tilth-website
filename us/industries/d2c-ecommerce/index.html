The challenge

Profitable ecommerce growth depends on the whole customer journey.

Paid acquisition can drive revenue while customer acquisition cost rises, creative fatigue accelerates or product pages fail to convert high-intent traffic. The answer is not always more media.

Tilth connects channel performance with creative learning, website behaviour, average order value, new-customer economics and repeat purchase considerations.

Economics

Acquisition economics

The useful performance view may combine spend, conversion rate, average order value, contribution, new-customer share and the time it takes for acquisition to create business value. The framework should reflect the brand's margin and repeat-purchase model.

Paid

Paid-media strategy

Paid channels should be selected and structured around the audience, product, offer and economics. Tilth can support Google, Meta and other approved channels where relevant to the scope.

Creative

Creative testing

Creative tests are organised around customer problem, product benefit, proof, offer, format and call to action. The goal is to create useful learning that informs both media and website decisions.

Conversion

Website and product-page conversion

The path from campaign to product page should maintain message continuity and answer the objections that influence purchase. We review hierarchy, product information, proof, friction, analytics and the next action.

Organic

SEO and organic discovery

Search can support category discovery, product research, comparison and branded demand. The system may include technical SEO, collection and product pages, commercial guides and internal linking.

Retention

Retention considerations

Even when the engagement does not include full lifecycle execution, acquisition decisions should consider new versus returning customers, repeat purchase, cohort behaviour and the difference between first-order return and longer-term value.

Measurement

Measurement and reporting

Reporting should help the team understand what changed across spend, traffic, product-page behaviour, checkout, purchase, average order value and customer mix. The exact model depends on the available data.

Working together

Distributed collaboration

US ecommerce teams can work with Tilth through agreed review rhythms, shared documentation and structured updates across time zones.
